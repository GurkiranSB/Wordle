Privacy Policy — Word Puzzle Coach

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Word Puzzle Coach is an offline puzzle helper. The app does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. Guesses and tile colors are processed on your device in memory and are not saved by the app. The bundled word list is read locally. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDK and requests no runtime permissions.

Information handled by the app

The app does not collect personal or sensitive user data. Information that you enter to use the solver stays in the app's current session on your device. It is not sent to the developer or other parties.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from children or anyone else. The app's intended audience is set by the developer in its Google Play listing.

Data retention and deletion

The app does not maintain user accounts or store guesses or tile feedback after the current app session. There is no account or server data to delete.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed version is distributed.
