# Privacy policy — Word Puzzle Coach

**Last updated:** October 15, 2026

Word Puzzle Coach is an offline puzzle helper. It does not ask you to create
an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.
Guesses and tile colors are processed on your device in memory and are not
saved by the app. The bundled word list is read locally. The app contains no
advertising or analytics SDK and requests no runtime permissions.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from children or from anyone
else. Choose the appropriate target-audience settings for the app in Google
Play Console.

## Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the
changed version is distributed.
